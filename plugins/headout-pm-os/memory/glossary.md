# Headout Full Glossary

Complete decoder ring for all internal terms, acronyms, shorthand, and nicknames.

## Product & Business Terms

| Term | Meaning | Context |
|------|---------|---------|
| MB / MBs | Minisite / Microsites | White-label branded sites (e.g., thevaticantickets.com, stonehenge-london-tours.com) |
| CE | City Experience | A managed product category tied to a venue or city activity |
| POI | Point of Interest | e.g., Vatican, Colosseum, Eiffel Tower |
| Non-POI | Non-Point of Interest | Experiences not anchored to a ticketed POI: cruises, day trips, whale watching, adventure |
| Hero CE | Hero City Experience | Top tier managed CE — 120 exist; highest revenue and investment |
| Pro+ CE | Pro-plus CE | Second tier — 350+ exist; proven but smaller scale |
| SP | Supply Partner | An operator who lists experiences on Headout |
| TID | Tour ID | A specific variant of a product (date/time/pax type specific) |
| TGID | Tour Group ID | A product/experience group containing multiple TIDs |
| VID | Vendor ID | A specific vendor/operator under a SP |
| pax | Passenger / participant | Pricing categories: adult, child, general, guest |
| Managed | Management tier | Full managed CE — curated assortment, multi-vendor, dedicated campaigns |
| Managed Lite | Management tier | Only hero TGID managed; selective intervention |
| Unmanaged | Management tier | Automated; systems-only management; long tail |
| MMP | Managed Marketplace Program | The end-to-end process for launching and managing a CE |
| ILF | Internal Listing Flow | Process by which new TGIDs get listed on Headout |
| distro | Distribution | Distribution channel or distribution pod |
| HNPL | Headout Now Pay Later | Deferred payment product; 8.74% net CVR lift on >4d lead time |
| Smart Pickups | Feature | Pickup logistics for non-POI tours; 9% net C2O lift |
| Guide Profiles | Feature | PM guide bios shown on select page; 10% CVR lift on dweb |
| Combos | Product type | Bundled experiences sold together |
| City Cards | Product type | City pass (e.g., Headout Barcelona/Dubai/London City Card) |
| HOHO | Hop-On Hop-Off | Bus tour product category |
| Day Trips | Product type | Excursions from a city to another destination |
| Drops | Feature | Flash sale / limited availability experience drops |
| Compass | Feature | LLM-based assistant for experience selection (piloting Dubai, London) |
| Frame | Feature | Photo tool for travelers |
| Headstart | Feature | Automated bookmarking via social media comment |
| Dex | Product | AI venue guide/companion — AR-based experience at venues; 3 live in London |

## CE Tier Buckets (Non-POI Strategy)

| Bucket | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| Mature | Post-PMF: POIs, central categories (Theatre, HOHO, City Cards, Airport Transfers). Scale to 100% coverage. |
| Growth | Early PMF: City Cruises, Day Trips to Ticketed POIs, Whale Watching, Adventure Activities, Must-Do/See. ~45% success rate. |
| Emerging | Seeking PMF: Day Trips w/o Ticketed POI, Day Trip Cruises. ~22% success rate. |
| Later | Weak PMF: City Tours, Food Tours, Classes/Workshops. De-prioritized. |
