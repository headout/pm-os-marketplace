# Headout — Company Context

## What Headout Does
Headout is a **managed experiences marketplace** — a platform for booking tours, attractions, and activities worldwide. Unlike open marketplaces (Viator, GetYourGuide), Headout actively manages ("curates") experiences end-to-end: curated supply, managed pricing, multi-vendor structure, and dedicated marketing.

**Core product:** Booking tickets & tours for experiences — POIs (Vatican, Colosseum, Eiffel Tower), Non-POIs (cruises, day trips, whale watching, walking tours, cooking classes), and live entertainment (theatre, HOHO).

## 2026 Strategy — The 4 Pillars

**North Star:** 50% YoY growth → $80M revenue H1 '26 | 10 Dex cities / 100 Dexs

### Pillar 1: Everything, Everywhere
Evolution from a narrowly managed marketplace to a ubiquitous tech-managed platform covering 10,000+ CEs via:
- **Managed** (~750 CEs) — full curation, multi-vendor, dedicated campaigns
- **Managed Lite** (~900 CEs) — hero TGID only, light touch
- **Unmanaged** (10,000+) — fully automated, systems-only

Enabled by **ExperienceOS** — the workflow engine connecting Growth, Content, BizOps, Catalog, Supplier integrations, and Marketing.

### Pillar 2: Non-POIs to the Moon
Target: $33M from non-POI CEs in 2026 (up from $13M in '25).
- **Growth bucket** (45% success rate): City Cruises, Day Trips to POIs, Whale Watching, Adventure, Must-Do/See → scale aggressively
- **Emerging bucket** (22% success rate): Day Trips w/o POI, Cruise Day Trips → selective PMF hunting
- **Later bucket**: City Tours, Food Tours, Classes → unmanaged for now

Product tailwinds: HNPL (8.74% CVR lift), Smart Pickups (9% C2O lift), Guide Profiles (10% CVR lift), Manual Date Selection (7% S2O lift on >4d lead time).

### Pillar 3: More Channels, More Momentum
$10M incremental revenue from new channels in '26:
- **B2B2C**: India (MakeMyTrip, TBO, TravClan), Loyalty (Miles & More, Revolut Rewards, Air India), Fintech (Scapia, HDFC Bank, Revolut Pay)
- **Creators**: 5K+ signed creators, 90M impressions — converting to measurable revenue (promo codes, own IP like Dex content, targeted campaigns)
- **Organic Search**: New CMS (Payload), LLM-powered content workflows, shoulder pages, non-POI organic push (target: 10%+ of non-POI revenue from organic)
- **Repeat / TOFU**: Product org owns $5M incremental repeat revenue; CRM base grew 10x to 1.5M (→ 15M by H1 EOY) via Iterable migration

### Pillar 4: Change Travel Forever with Dex
Dex is now an **emerging business unit** at Headout (no longer a moonshot — Dex is real and shipping).
- **What it is:** AI audio guide / companion for venues, neighborhoods, and cities (AR + AI). Initially venue-focused; expanding to neighborhood Dexs and city Dexs
- **Scale targets:** 100 Dexs by H1, 500–1,000 by EOY
- **Revenue target:** $10M from Dex in '26, with 3x run-rate going into '27
- **Three vectors:** HO Halo (lift on existing business), Venue Partnerships (B2B), B2C (standalone acquisition)
- **Leadership:** Rachit + Varun as central PoCs; Neil leads Studio (product + eng); Prasenjit leads User App (product + eng); Ekansh is GTM PoC (with Aman K contributing); Mukul on Dex product design, Sakshi on Dex GTM design; Viraj leads production scalability

## Business Context

- **2025 growth:** 32% overall; 50%+ when excluding supply-impacted CEs

- **Mission:** "The definitive way to head out" — building towards market leadership and IPO-worthiness
