## Spillover Patterns (Signals for PRD Scope Quality)

Recurring spillover reasons observed:
1. **Undiscovered edge cases at time of spec** (schedule management spilled Q2→Q3→Q4→Q1'26)
2. **3rd party dependency delays** (TikTok issue; Paypal negotiations blocking PPL)
3. **Under-resourced FE** (Signup form at risk in Q1'26; app parity always lagging)
4. **Data analysis taking longer than planned** (multiple experiments listed as "data being analysed" for 6+ weeks)
5. **Scope expansion mid-quarter** (CMS migration underestimated — spilled 4 quarters)
6. **Solutioning done but not picked up** (many items hit "Solutioning done" state, then wait for bandwidth)

> **PM OS implication:** PRDs should explicitly call out 3rd party dependencies, FE vs BE separation, and experiment analysis buffer time. These are the top spillover causes.
